how to read this deckThe visual key
Legend

Six ways of drawing one return.

Borrowed from the grammar of Schwabish, Lima, Tufte and Lupi — every chart in this deck uses one of these six encodings, and nothing is decorative.

Sunburst
Angle is share of income. Ring depth is how far a dollar travels from its source.
Ribbon flow
Width equals dollars. Where a ribbon narrows, a deduction removed money from the taxable base.
Waffle
One hundred squares, one per percent. Fill tells you what share of a total was actually usable.
Glyph
Each move gets a petal per attribute: size = dollars, spokes = effort, ring = deadline pressure.
Arc calendar
Twelve months bent into a circle. Radius marks the deadline; the arc shows how long you have.
Cascade
Each bar is one decision. The floor drops step by step from what you paid to what you could pay.

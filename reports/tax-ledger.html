Bob & Sally Jones · MFJ13 — Observations
What the return says when you read it as a document rather than a bill

Observations from the 2025 return

Opportunity

Roth is closed at the front door, open at the back

MAGI of $357,058 sits far above the $246,000 contribution ceiling. Neither spouse holds a pre-tax IRA balance, so a $16,000 backdoor contribution is available with no pro-rata cost.

Opportunity

No HSA on a return that should have one

The family plan qualifies and both spouses are over 55. A $9,550 contribution is the only deduction on the form that escapes tax at contribution, growth and withdrawal.

Watch

Municipal interest is raising your Medicare premium

$6,000 of tax-exempt interest is added back for IRMAA and lifts 2027 premiums a full tier — roughly $2,900 across two enrollees. Taxable yield may net out ahead.

Watch

Only 30% of dividends arrive qualified

$6,900 of the $9,804 dividend total is taxed at 24% plus 3.8% rather than 15%. This is a fund-selection and location question, not a planning one.

Opportunity

2025 was an itemising year; 2026 may not be

Without the $88,383 donor-advised gift, deductions fall to $36,000 against a $30,000 standard. Bunching two years of giving into alternating years preserves the advantage.

Watch

$23,000 of capital loss is idle

Carried forward at $3,000 a year it takes eight years to absorb. Paired with the Scenario 2 exercise it is consumed immediately and saves $3,450.

Opportunity

The 24% bracket has $151,225 of unused room

Between today's bracket and the projected RMD rate there is a durable spread. Filling that room annually is worth more than any single large conversion.

Watch

SALT cap wastes $3,423 of tax paid

While California residency continues, the S-corp can elect the pass-through entity tax and deduct the state liability at the entity level instead.

Clear

No AMT, no additional Medicare tax, no penalty

AMT exposure is $0 with the state deduction already capped, earned income is $28,372 below the surtax threshold, and withholding met the 90% test.

Make the PTE election on SCO Side Biz.

California permits S-corps and partnerships to pay a 9.3% elective tax at the entity level on qualified income. The entity deducts it federally and shareholders claim a California credit — bypassing the $10K cap on that slice of state tax.

Why this beats a SEP-IRA.

John & Jane Doe · Estate23 — Glossary
Every term used in this report

Glossary

Applicable exclusion
The amount you can transfer free of gift and estate tax. $15.0M per person in 2026.
Basis step-up
Assets in a taxable estate are revalued at death, erasing built-in capital gain.
Domicile
Your permanent legal home. Determines which state can tax your whole estate.
Fiduciary
Anyone acting for someone else’s benefit: trustee, executor, agent under a power.
GST tax
A second 40% tax on transfers that skip a generation. Has its own exclusion.
Marital deduction
Unlimited deduction for transfers to a citizen spouse. Defers tax, never erases it.
Portability
Lets a surviving spouse use the deceased spouse’s unused exclusion. Must be elected on Form 706.
QTIP
A marital trust that pays income to the spouse while you control who receives it after.
§7520 rate
The IRS discount rate used to value split-interest gifts. Published monthly.
Situs
Where an asset is legally located. Real property is taxed by its situs state.
Skip person
A grandchild or anyone two or more generations below you.
Zeroed-out
A GRAT structured so the reported taxable gift is effectively nil.
